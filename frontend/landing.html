Your instinct is right, but your timeline is doing the arguing for you. The idea is worth building — the six-week launch is the part that will hurt you.
Here's what I'm hearing

You've built something you believe in, a few people confirmed it, and now the waiting feels heavier than the risk.

What you might be assuming
  • That "everyone said it was cool" is the same signal as "someone would pay for this."
  • That going full-time removes the obstacle — when the obstacle might be distribution, not hours.
Where I'd gently push back
  • Ten thousand users in month one from TikTok isn't a plan, it's a hope with a number attached.
What's genuinely working
  • You shipped a prototype in two weekends. That's real evidence about you, not just the idea.
What I'd try instead

Keep the job for eight more weeks and try to find twenty students who'll use it weekly. If you can't find twenty while employed, full-time won't fix that — it'll just make it more expensive to learn.
